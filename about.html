Helpful Researcher Tips

  • While our department does not provide in-depth archival research for patrons, our archivists can schedule individualized consultations to discuss our primary source collections, suggest relevant materials for your research, and recommend search strategies. We can also digitize a limited amount of targeted material within our established reproduction and fee framework.
  • To ensure a timely response to questions, please use our general contact form rather than emailing individual archivists directly. All department staff have access to these submissions and can assist with inquiries as appropriate.
  • The more specific your research inquiry, the easier it is for an archivist to assist you. If your request involves a large amount of material on a broad topic, it may be too general for us to fulfill through our remote reference and digitization services. In such cases, you may need to visit in person to conduct more extensive research, hire an independent researcher at your own expense, or narrow and/or prioritize the scope of your request so that archivists can digitize selected material for you within the reproduction and fee framework. Our bookmarks feature allows you to conveniently make your selections for remote requests.
  • Our department receives many remote reference inquiries each week. Because of this volume, we are unable to offer expedited digitization services. Please plan your requests accordingly and contact our department well in advance of any deadlines. Our typical turnaround time for an initial response is one to two weeks, depending on the complexity of the inquiry. If the inquiry subsequently requires payment for digitization services, these orders are added to our reproduction queue in the order that payment is received. Some materials require preservation review and treatment or special handling which extends the reproduction time frame.
  • Researchers are responsible for taking careful notes, photographs, and citations during their visits to the department. Archives staff cannot subsequently verify citations or identify the locations of material in collections based upon general descriptions. If a researcher requests reproductions following an in-person visit, they must provide specific location information for the requested item(s), including the collection, series, box, folders, or other relevant identifying information.
