AI Guidance and Research Services

The M.E. Grenander Department of Special Collections & Archives receives many reference requests from patrons drafted with the assistance of AI chatbots. While generative AI can be helpful as a starting point for research, patrons should also be mindful that we manage tens of thousands of boxes of records and that archivists may only be able to meet focused or targeted requests.

If you receive a reference to one of our Department's resources from an AI chatbot, please remember that it may not be correct. AI chatbots do not fact check, which can lead to them generating misleading, inaccurate, or fabricated references.

The most accurate information sources for our department are the finding aids to our collections, the University Library catalog for our rare books, and Department staff.

Targeted inquiries about specific portions of materials are more likely to be fulfilled remotely. We are unable to perform detailed research, so broad research questions will likely require in-person research, unless they concern the few collections listed in our Selections, which are mostly digitized.

If you are prompted by an AI chatbot to request an expansive amount of material related to a topic, be aware that it may be too vague a reference inquiry to fulfill, not possible to fulfill because the sources do not exist, or well beyond the remote reference and digitization services of our Department. You may need to [visit in person](https://archives.albany.edu/visit/) to conduct this in-depth research or hire an independent researcher at your own expense.

We look forward to working with you on your research needs.
